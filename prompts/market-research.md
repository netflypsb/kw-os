# Market Research

Research the market for [TOPIC] and create a competitive landscape analysis:

1. **Define Scope**: Clarify the market segment, geography, and timeframe.
2. **Search**: Use web-search to find industry reports, articles, and data.
3. **Deep Dive**: Use playwright to access detailed pages, extract data tables, pricing info.
4. **Competitors**: Identify top 5-10 players. Visit their websites. Extract features, pricing, positioning.
5. **Data**: Build comparison matrix in Excel (features, pricing, market share, strengths, weaknesses).
6. **Analyze**: Use sequentialthinking to identify market gaps, trends, and opportunities.
7. **Visualize**: Create market map chart, feature comparison chart, market share pie chart.
8. **Report**: Generate PowerPoint with market overview, competitive landscape, opportunities, recommendations.
9. **Save**: Save to `reports/market-research/[topic]-YYYY-MM-DD/`.

**Parameters**: [topic/industry] [geography] [specific focus areas]
